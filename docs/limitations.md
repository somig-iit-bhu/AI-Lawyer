# Limitations

- Text extraction quality is not equivalent to legal completeness.
- Heading and section detection is deterministic and may need document-specific review.
- No OCR, reranker, verified source URL mapping, legal consolidation, or legal advice is implemented.

- The optional Phase 7 features (voice input, rental-dispute workflow, user document upload, and opposing-party simulation) remain disabled by feature flags and are not implemented.
- Legal response quality cannot be claimed until the selected embedding model, source mapping, and generation provider are evaluated on fresh inputs.
- A generated draft is a reviewable starting point, not a filed legal document or professional legal advice.
